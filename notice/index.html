  • September 14, 2026
    Paper accepted to IEEE S&P 2027

    Our paper “The Illusion of Provenance: An Empirical Study of Package Attestation Across Software Ecosystems” has been accepted to the 48th IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy (IEEE S&P 2027).

    Authors: Sangkyoung Park, Jiyong Lee, and Hyunsoo Kwon.

    The paper examines what package attestation actually establishes across software ecosystems, and finds that the guarantee behind a “verified provenance” indicator differs a great deal between registries.

    Congratulations to Sangkyoung and Jiyong on their first top-tier paper.

  • May 1, 2026
    Survey published in ACM Computing Surveys

    Our survey “Certificate Revocation in the TLS Ecosystem: A Survey” appears in ACM Computing Surveys, Vol. 58, No. 7.

    Authors: Hyunsoo Kwon, Doowon Kim, Hodong Kim, Changhee Hahn, and Junbeom Hur.

    The survey traces how certificate revocation has been handled in practice, and why decades of partial fixes have left the mechanism weaker than the specifications suggest.

  • October 13, 2025
    Paper presented at ACM CCS 2025

    Our paper “Deep Dive into In-app Browsers: Uncovering Hidden Pitfalls in Certificate Validation” was presented at the ACM Conference on Computer and Communications Security (ACM CCS 2025) in Taipei, Taiwan.

    Authors: Woonghee Lee, Junbeom Hur, and Hyunsoo Kwon.

    The paper measures how the embedded webviews inside messaging and social apps validate TLS certificates, and shows that validation there is weaker than in the standalone browser on the same device.
